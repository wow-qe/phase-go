# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus debate
on ideas and evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and sexualized conduct are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers will
review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may edit
or remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban serious or repeated violations.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project.
